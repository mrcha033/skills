# Output contract

Produce the answer in Korean unless the user requests another language.

## 1. Bottom line

State the strongest defensible conclusion in one paragraph. Label it `확정`, `논증 가능`, or `미확정`.

## 2. Controlling route

Identify:

- the controlling governance domain (`club_union` or `student_council`) and why;
- the competent body;
- the applicable date and rule version;
- the agenda or procedural route;
- quorum, seconding, and voting thresholds;
- the remedy the body may actually adopt.

Attach `[P:p_###]` after every material proposition and `[S:source_id]` after every authority citation.

## 3. Argument map

Give:

- the user's thesis;
- each rule premise;
- each fact premise;
- the inference connecting them;
- the requested conclusion or motion.

Do not disguise policy arguments as rules.

## 4. Strongest counterargument

State the best adverse reading and cite it. Then answer it. If it cannot be answered, narrow the requested conclusion.

## 5. Meeting-ready language

Provide the requested speech, motion, amendment, or objection. Make the exact operative text and denominator explicit.

## 6. Unknowns and evidence needed

List only unknowns that could change the result. State what document, count, or official interpretation would resolve each.

## 7. Sources

For each source include organization, date, title, article/page, and direct URL. Separate:

- controlling internal rules;
- official interpretations or decisions;
- minutes and factual records;
- external Korean law;
- user-supplied evidence.

Never cite an unresolved search snippet as the governing text.
Never combine the total student council's rule with the club union's rule as if they were one hierarchy. Explain any cross-domain authority or delegation explicitly.
